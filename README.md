# AI-Driven Decision Framework for Smart Manufacturing under Industry 5.0

## Overview

This repository contains the research assets, dataset, analytical outputs, and framework developed for my undergraduate dissertation:

**"Impact of AI Models in Smart Electronics Manufacturing under Industry 5.0: Developing a Framework for Product Personalization"**

The study investigates how Artificial Intelligence (AI) maturity influences manufacturing performance and customer satisfaction through key Industry 5.0 capabilities including Quality Control, Predictive Maintenance, Customization & Personalization, Human–AI Collaboration, and Sustainability & Resilience.

Using **Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)** in **SmartPLS**, the research develops and validates the **Smart Manufacturing Excellence Framework for Personalization (SMEF-P)**.

## Research Objectives

The study aimed to:

* Assess the impact of AI Maturity on manufacturing capabilities.
* Examine the role of Quality Control and Predictive Maintenance in AI-enabled manufacturing.
* Evaluate how Human–AI Collaboration contributes to organizational performance.
* Investigate Sustainability and Resilience as drivers of customer-centric outcomes.
* Develop an evidence-based decision framework for Industry 5.0 manufacturing environments.

## Key Findings

### Significant Findings

* AI Maturity positively influences Quality Control capability.
* AI Maturity positively influences Predictive Maintenance capability.
* Human–AI Collaboration plays a critical role in translating technical capabilities into organizational outcomes.
* Sustainability and Resilience significantly contribute to Customer Personalization Satisfaction.

### Managerial Insight

The results suggest that organizations cannot rely solely on technological advancement to create customer value. Successful Industry 5.0 implementation requires a combination of:

* AI capability development
* Human-centered design
* Workforce collaboration
* Sustainable operational practices
